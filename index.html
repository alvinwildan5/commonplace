Commonplace

Write down a thought before it disappears.

Some thoughts arrive quietly. They do not ask to be remembered, yet for a brief moment, they make the world feel different. Then time moves, another thought takes its place, and what once felt important becomes impossible to recall.

Perhaps that is why we write.

Even the word desa feels closer to Indonesia than kota. Perhaps that is why Desanesia sounds more familiar than Kotanesia.

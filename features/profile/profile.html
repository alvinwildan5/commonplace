Bridging Science and Community

I am driven by the intersection of climate dynamics, remote sensing, and sustainable policy. With an academic foundation in Applied Meteorology from IPB University, my work heavily involves processing multi-sensor satellite imagery and formulating spatial models to monitor environmental changes, particularly in vital ecosystems like mangroves.

Beyond technical analysis using Google Earth Engine and Python, I believe that data must translate into actionable community resilience. Whether it is engaging in international climate policy frameworks or grassroots disaster mitigation projects, my goal is to foster informed decision-making for a changing world.
